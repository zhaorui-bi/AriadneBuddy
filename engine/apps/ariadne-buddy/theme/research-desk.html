How this desk works
Round
A round is one pass of the loop from hypothesis to validation. The controller opens a new round only when you choose to iterate.
Version
Every decision advances the task version. If another surface records a decision first, the server answers 409 and the desk reloads instead of overwriting.
Request revision
A revision identifies one engine request. Artifacts and jobs are filed under the revision that produced them.
Gates
The loop stops at a gate whenever a human decision is required. Decisions are recorded as caller-declared with a written reason.
